## 1. Introduction

Welcome to TBM (The Building Market). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Platform").

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the Platform.

This policy complies with the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019**, issued by the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), and applicable international data protection standards.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account information:** Full name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never store your plain-text password).
- **Profile information:** Delivery addresses, phone number, and any profile details you choose to add.
- **Payment information:** When you make a purchase, payment is processed by **Paystack** (our payment provider). TBM does not store your card number, CVV, or full bank details — only a transaction reference and status.
- **Room and design photos:** Images you upload to our AI Visualizer feature for generating renovation designs.
- **Messages:** Conversations you have with ZIORA, our AI assistant.
- **Renovation estimates:** Project details (room type, dimensions, finish level) you provide when using the Renovation Estimator.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information:** Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
- **Usage data:** Pages viewed, features used, session duration, and interaction logs.
- **Location data:** General location (city/state level) derived from your IP address or, if you grant permission, your device's GPS — used only to improve delivery estimates and product availability.
- **Log data:** IP address, app crash reports, and error logs for debugging and security purposes.

### 2.3 Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect biometric data.
- We do not access your contacts, microphone, or camera beyond what is needed to upload a photo you explicitly select.
- We do not track your activity across third-party apps or websites.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 Service Providers (Data Processors)
We engage trusted third-party service providers to operate the Platform. Each is bound by data processing agreements:

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Paystack** | Payment processing | Nigeria / Global |
| **Cloudinary** | Storage and delivery of uploaded images and AI-generated designs | USA |

### 4.2 Vendors on the Platform
When you place an order, relevant order details (delivery address, items ordered) are shared with the fulfilling vendor. Vendors do not receive your payment details.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information where required by Nigerian law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TBM, our users, or the public.

### 4.4 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 5. AI Features — Special Notice

### 5.1 AI Visualizer (Room Design)
When you upload a room photo, it is transmitted to **Replicate** (an AI generation service) to produce renovation visualisations. Your original photo and the generated output are stored in **Cloudinary**. Do not upload photos containing sensitive personal information or images of people without their consent.

### 5.2 ZIORA AI Assistant
Conversations with ZIORA are stored on our servers to maintain conversation history and improve response quality. Conversation content is processed by **Groq's** language model API. Do not share sensitive financial or personally identifying details (e.g., bank account numbers, passwords) in the chat.

### 5.3 AI Credit System
Your AI generation credit balance is stored on our servers and tied to your account. Credits are consumed when you generate designs and are non-refundable except as required by applicable consumer protection law.

## 7. Your Rights

Under the NDPR and applicable law, you have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Deletion** — Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention obligations).
- **Objection** — Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdrawal of consent** — Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.
- **Data portability** — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Lodge a complaint** — File a complaint with NITDA (Nigeria's data protection authority) at nitda.gov.ng.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@tbmbuilding.com**. We will respond within **30 days**.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.
- **Encryption at rest:** Sensitive data fields are encrypted in our database.
- **Password hashing:** Passwords are stored using strong cryptographic hashing — we cannot read your password.
- **JWT authentication:** Sessions use short-lived signed tokens with secure refresh mechanisms.
- **Access controls:** Internal access to user data is restricted on a need-to-know basis.

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you and NITDA within 72 hours as required by the NDPR.
